About Me

I am an applied mathematician developing data-driven reduced-order models for complex multiscale systems. My research combines score-based generative modeling, stochastic dynamics, response theory, and neural differential equations to construct computationally efficient models that preserve observed statistics, temporal correlations, and responses to perturbations, with applications in fluid dynamics, geophysics, and statistical physics.

Research

Score-Based Stochastic Reduced-Order Models

Markovian closures that reproduce prescribed stationary distributions and dynamical correlations directly from data, without repeated solver-in-the-loop calibration.

Response Theory and Parameter Calibration

Generalized fluctuation-dissipation methods for predicting forced responses and estimating sensitivities of statistical observables from unperturbed trajectories.

Multiscale and Chaotic Dynamics

Hybrid Langevin and neural-ODE models that combine accurate long-time statistics with short-time prediction of rare transitions in multiscale systems.
